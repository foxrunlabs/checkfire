CHECKFIRE / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

CheckFire is developed by Ryan Clarke at Fox Run Labs. This policy explains how the app handles your information and what happens if you contact us for support.

Information collection

CheckFire performs calculations on your device. The app does not automatically collect or transmit your paycheck scenarios, financial inputs, or personal information to the developer. It does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

CheckFire stores paycheck scenarios locally using Apple’s SwiftData framework. Depending on what you enter, this may include:

  • A paycheck name and pay period.
  • Military pay grade and years of service.
  • Basic pay, allowances, special pay, incentive pay, and bonuses.
  • Deductions and allotments, including names and amounts.
  • Traditional and Roth Thrift Savings Plan elections.
  • Federal withholding selections, dependent counts, other income, deductions, and extra withholding.

The app also stores preferences such as sorting options. Saved information supports calculations and allows you to reopen scenarios.

Backups and deletion

Local information may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your Apple settings. Backups are managed by Apple under its own policies and account settings; the developer does not receive them.

Delete individual paycheck scenarios or use Settings → Delete All Data to remove saved scenarios. Deleting the app removes local app data; offloading may retain it. Removing scenarios does not necessarily remove copies already held in separately managed backups.

There is no CheckFire server-side account or paycheck database for the developer to access or delete.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

CheckFire does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies. It does not use the advertising identifier or track you across apps and websites. The developer does not sell your paycheck information.

External links

The app links to support, this policy, the project repository, and official reference material. Opening a link may communicate with the website’s operator. External websites and services handle information under their own policies.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond and provide support. Correspondence may be retained as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations.

You do not need to provide an actual LES or identifying financial records to request help. Remove sensitive information from examples and screenshots. GitHub issues and attachments may be publicly visible and are handled under GitHub’s policies.

For support, privacy questions, or requests concerning support correspondence, email labs@thefoxrun.net.

Security

Keeping paycheck information on the device limits the data the developer receives. Security also depends on your device passcode, operating system, Apple account, and backup settings.

Children’s privacy

CheckFire is not directed at children. The app does not automatically collect personal information from its users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its information practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Independent application

CheckFire is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the U.S. Department of Defense, Defense Finance and Accounting Service, Internal Revenue Service, or any other government agency.
