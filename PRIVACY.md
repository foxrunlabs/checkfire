# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026

CheckFire is designed to perform its calculations locally on your device and to collect as little information as possible.

This Privacy Policy describes how CheckFire handles information when you use the application.

## Information Collection

**CheckFire does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or with third parties.**

The application does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry services.

CheckFire does not collect information such as:

- Your name, email address, or other contact information
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Usage or analytics data
- Advertising identifiers
- Financial information entered into the application
- Diagnostic or telemetry information

## Information Stored on Your Device

CheckFire allows you to create and save paycheck scenarios containing information used to estimate military compensation, deductions, taxes, retirement contributions, and net pay.

Depending on how you use the application, this information may include items such as:

- Military pay grade and years of service
- Basic pay and other compensation
- Allowances, special pays, incentive pays, and bonuses
- Deductions and allotments
- Thrift Savings Plan contribution settings
- Federal income tax withholding selections and related values

This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

CheckFire does not transmit this information to Fox Run Labs, the developer, or any external server.

Information stored by CheckFire may be included in device backups according to your Apple device and iCloud backup settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policies and account settings.

## Third-Party Services

CheckFire does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, authentication, or cloud-storage services.

The application may provide links to external resources, including websites operated by agencies such as the U.S. Department of Defense, Defense Finance and Accounting Service, or Internal Revenue Service. If you choose to open an external link, your interaction with that website is governed by the privacy policy of the organization operating it.

## Tracking

CheckFire does not track users across applications or websites.

CheckFire does not access Apple's advertising identifier or use information for targeted advertising.

## Data Sharing

Because CheckFire does not collect or receive your personal information, the developer does not sell, rent, disclose, or otherwise share your information with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Paycheck scenarios and other user-created information are stored locally on your device.

You may remove individual information using the application's available deletion controls. Removing CheckFire from your device also removes the application's locally stored data, subject to any device or cloud backups maintained separately by the operating system.

Because CheckFire does not transmit user information to the developer, there is no server-side account or personal-data record for the developer to delete.

## Children's Privacy

CheckFire is not designed to collect personal information from children or any other users. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

CheckFire minimizes privacy and security risk by keeping user-entered information on the device and avoiding external collection or transmission of that information.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security features and settings of your Apple device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if CheckFire's features or data practices change.

Material changes to the way the application collects, uses, stores, or shares information will be reflected in an updated version of this policy. The effective date at the top of the document will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
